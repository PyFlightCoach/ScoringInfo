# Your profile

Edit your details and password.

- Update First Name, Last Name, Country and **About Me**, then press **Update**. Your email address cannot be changed here.
- If your email is unverified a link to request a new verification email is shown.
- Change your password in the second form (minimum ten characters) with **Update Password**.
- To delete your account, email the address shown at the bottom of the page.

[Full instructions](https://pyflightcoach.github.io/FCScoreDocs/instructions/users/)
