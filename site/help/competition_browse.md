# Browse competitions

Lists the competitions you can see. Use it to open a competition or to enter one.

- Use the buttons above the table to filter: **All**, **Entered**, **Open** and, for contest directors, **Mine**.
- Use the **Category** selector to limit the list to one schedule category.
- **View** opens a competition (**Edit** for competitions you run). **Enter** adds you as a competitor where the competition allows it.

For how competitions work, see [Competitions](https://pyflightcoach.github.io/FCScoreDocs/instructions/competitions/)
