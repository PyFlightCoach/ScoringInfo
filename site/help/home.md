# Welcome to Flight Coach Score

Use the navigation bar at the top of the page to choose what to do. You do not need an account to analyse a flight, but you need one to share flights and use the leaderboards.

- **User menu**: Login, Sign Up, Profile and logout.
- **Flight menu**: **Create** loads a flight log (ArduPilot BIN or AcroWRX file), **Import** loads a BIN with a saved analysis, **Load Template** opens an empty sequence and **Example** shows a worked example. Once a flight is loaded the menu also offers Box, Manoeuvres and Results.
- **Database menu** (logged in): **Leaderboard** and **Schedules**.
- **Competition menu** (logged in): browse, view and enter competitions.
- **Help button**: enabled when help is available for the page you are on.

When you are logged in this page also shows news and tables of your recent flights, top flights and activity.

[Full instructions](https://pyflightcoach.github.io/FCScoreDocs/instructions/)
