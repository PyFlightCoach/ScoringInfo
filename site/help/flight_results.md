# Flight and manoeuvre results

This page serves the whole sequence and each manoeuvre.

## Sequence view
- The **Sequence Results** menu lists each manoeuvre with its K factor and score, and the total (with percentage) at the bottom. Click a row to open that manoeuvre.
- **Info** shows flight details (source, pilot, schedule, wind estimate) and the analysis settings.
- **Upload** shares the flight (see below). **Analysis** sets **Analysis version**, **Difficulty** (Hard, Moderate, Easy) and **Truncate downgrades**, and has **Run all analyses**.
- If a recording holds several sequences or repeats, choose which to view or score in the lists at the top.
- **Compare Flight** overlays another flight from the database.
- For AcroWRX flights, **Adjust box** and **Edit splits** return to those pages.

## Manoeuvre view
- **Back to sequence** returns; the previous/next buttons step through manoeuvres.
- The **Intra**, **Inter** and **Box** buttons show the score for each group, plus **Total**. Select one to list its downgrades; click a downgrade to see its description and plot.
- Intra downgrades are grouped; click a group to open it. Box lists only the downgrades that cost marks.
- The downgrade panel shows the judging description first, then a plot of the measurement, or a table for centring.
- The gear menu has **Auto-advance to the next manoeuvre**, **Show active element downgrades**, **Show template** and **Show comparison flight**.
- **Adjust segmentation** opens the **Element Segmentation** menu: drag a plane to move the boundary between elements, then **Score current boundaries**, **Optimise from current boundaries** or **Reset boundary changes**. Only needed if segmentation has gone wrong.

## Upload
You must be logged in with a verified email and the analysis must be complete. Choose **Privacy** (basic, view flown, view analysis), add **Comments**, then upload. If it cannot be uploaded, the reason is shown.
