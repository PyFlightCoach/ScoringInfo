# Sign up

Create an account to upload flights and use the leaderboards.

- Fill in First Name, Last Name, **Email address**, **Password** (minimum ten characters) and confirm it, and **Country**.
- Read the Terms of Use (**View Terms of Use**) and tick to accept, then press **Register**.
- You will be emailed a link to verify your address. You cannot upload until you have verified it.

[Full instructions](https://pyflightcoach.github.io/FCScoreDocs/instructions/users/)
